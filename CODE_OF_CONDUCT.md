# Community Code of Conduct — Pathixo Rewards

Pathixo Rewards is committed to providing a friendly, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who contributes to or interacts with the project.

---

## 1. Expected Behavior

We encourage all contributors and maintainers to:
- Use welcoming, professional, and inclusive language.
- Show empathy and respect toward different viewpoints and experiences.
- Graciously accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project, community, and users.

---

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable in project spaces:
- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information of others without explicit consent.
- Inappropriate use of sexualized language or imagery.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

---

## 3. Enforcement and Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project administrators at:

- **Email**: `conduct@pathixo.com`

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and investigated fairly. Project maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
