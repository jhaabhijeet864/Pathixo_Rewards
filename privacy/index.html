Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Core Philosophy: Zero Prompt Reading

Pathixo Rewards is architected with strict zero-data harvesting. The browser extension operates in an isolated content script environment that never reads, captures, stores, or logs your user prompt text, uploaded files, or the generated answers from any AI model (including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, or DeepSeek).

2. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

Pathixo Rewards strictly complies with the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only request the storage permission to persist anonymous device credentials and cached ad configurations locally.
  • We do not request or access <all_urls>, browsing history, cookies, or tabs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers.

3. What We Collect and Why

To operate the rewards and impressions accounting system, the extension logs strictly:

  • Anonymous Device Identifier: A random client-generated UUID used to route micro-earnings to your account.
  • Impression Verification Tokens: An anonymized event record confirming that an ad was displayed on screen for at least 4 continuous seconds during model thinking.
  • Payout Destination: When you request a cashout (UPI ID, PayPal email, or delivery email), this is used exclusively to transfer your money.

4. Open Source Transparency

Our complete browser extension codebase is public and open-source on GitHub at https://github.com/jhaabhijeet864/Pathixo_Rewards. You and independent security researchers can inspect and verify every DOM query and network call.
